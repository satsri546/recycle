How to Recycle and Sort

  • 🗂️ Segregate waste into categories — plastics, paper, glass, metals, e-waste, organic, or hazardous.
  • 💧 Clean the item. Rinse bottles, jars, and cans to remove food or liquid residue.
  • 📦 Sort items properly. Keep paper dry, separate glass by color if required, and flatten cardboard boxes.
  • 🗑️ Place recyclables in the correct bin. Compost organic waste separately, and take e-waste to certified centers.
  • 🚫 Avoid contamination. Do not mix non-recyclables like Styrofoam or greasy pizza boxes with recyclables.
  • 📍 Check local guidelines. Recycling rules vary by city.

Recycling Guide

  • ♻️ Plastics: Rinse bottles, recycle #1 and #2 plastics.
  • 📄 Paper: Newspapers, cardboard, and office paper (keep dry).
  • 🍶 Glass: Rinse jars and bottles, separate by color if required.
  • 🥫 Metals: Aluminum and tin cans are recyclable after rinsing.
  • 💻 E-Waste: Phones, laptops, and batteries → certified centers.
  • 🌱 Organic: Banana peels and food scraps → compost.
  • 🗑️ Non-recyclables: Styrofoam, greasy pizza boxes, plastic bags.
